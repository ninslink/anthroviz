AIMM · Artificial Intelligence for Monitoring Malnutrition

Low-cost, image-based nutrition monitoring for rural households and public decision-making.

AnthroViz translates the AIMM approach into a visual anthropometry system: smartphone images, privacy-preserving capture, segmentation, pose estimation and AI-assisted anthropometric inference for near-real-time monitoring.

Core objectives

Maximise measurement accuracy

Refine deep-learning models estimating MUAC, WHZ and related anthropometric indicators from 2D and IR images; validate internally, externally and in field settings.

Maximise community usability

Support rural caregivers and ASHA-linked workflows through low-cost phones, non-invasive capture, multilingual/audio guidance and iterative household feedback.

Impact pathway

Household

Caregivers self-monitor children and receive timely feedback.

Community

ASHA workers support trust, training and early action capacity.

Policy

Aggregated data support district/state targeting and SDG monitoring.

Design principles

Privacy by design

IR imaging and on-device anonymisation before upload.

Open science

Open-source or dual licensing posture for affordability and reuse.

Equity checks

Subgroup validation, district diversity and recalibration if gaps appear.
